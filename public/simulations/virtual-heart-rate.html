      Try it on yourself

      Find your pulse: put two fingers (not your thumb) on the inside of your other wrist, below the thumb. Feel the tiny beats.

      1. Sit quietly. Count your pulse for 10 seconds.
      2. March gently on the spot for 30 seconds.
      3. Count again. Straight away, count your pulse for 10 seconds.
      4. Rest and drink water. Then see your result.

      Note for parents and carers: this optional activity is gentle and should be done with an adult nearby. Children can complete the whole lab without moving.
